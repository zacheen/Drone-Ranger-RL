# V1 timeout bootstrap

Ten unseeded Stage 0 training runs with default settings. Each checkpoint was scored greedily on the first episode of 2000 seeded layouts (seed 1). The best checkpoint of a run was picked on a separate 1000-layout set (seed 0).

Failure modes split the final checkpoint's failed episodes. Oscillate means at least 10% of steps reverse the previous step's turn. Zigzag means at least 5% of steps turn against the last turn with forward steps in between. Spin means at least 90% of steps turn. Orbit is a timeout that ends within 2 m of the target. Each is a share of all 2000 episodes.
